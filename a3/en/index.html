Our services

Services

Our team of experts covers every area of surveying engineering, including:

We provide a full range of survey documentation – from updating cadastral data to registration in the land register – to ensure accurate and lawful property records. Croatia has several types of cadastral survey reports, each with a specific purpose. Here is a short overview of the main types:

  • Subdivision survey report

    Used to subdivide existing parcels into smaller ones or to merge several parcels into one or more new parcels.

    Learn more

  • Building registration

    Used to register buildings (houses, farm buildings and other structures) in the cadastre and land register. Includes a detailed survey and a review of occupancy permits so that your property is recorded consistently in all registers.

    Learn more

  • Survey report for changes in parcel shape or area

    This survey corrects the area and shape of your parcel and records its actual state in the cadastre and land register.

    Learn more

  • Complete legalisation documentation

    Legalisation of unpermitted buildings consists of a survey report and an architectural/design report. It contains precisely measured data on the position, area and height of the building and enables an occupancy decision to be issued.

    Learn more

  • Topographic survey for house and building design

    Surveyors are the first and the last on a building site. A topographic base map – a 3D terrain model – is a prerequisite for design by licensed architects and engineers. Once permits are issued, we follow your construction site through several stages.

    Learn more

  • Condominium division (etažiranje)

    We prepare condominium division documentation for residential and commercial buildings, enabling a property to be divided precisely into individual units, so owners and investors can register their flats as separate properties.

    Learn more

  • Real estate brokerage

    In addition to classic brokerage, our team combines expertise in cadastral surveying, which allows us to offer you far more than a standard agency service.

    Learn more

Special services

  • Engineering surveying (construction-site surveying)

    Our experts provide surveying support on construction sites, precisely setting out foundations, structures, roads, tunnels and other works so that every element matches the design.

    Learn more

  • Aerial photogrammetry (drones)

    We use state-of-the-art aerial survey technology with drones, giving fast and accurate insight into the terrain for mapping, volume monitoring and site investigation.

    Learn more

  • 3D laser scanning

    Laser scanning lets us deliver detailed 3D models of buildings and terrain, giving clients precise 3D visualisation and analysis of every aspect of a project – from refurbishing existing buildings and cultural heritage to terrain and urban infrastructure models.

    Learn more

  • GIS (geographic information systems)

    Collection, processing, analysis and visualisation of spatial data. Digital maps, spatial analysis and georeferencing for your projects.
