Engineering surveying

Engineering surveying – surveying services on construction sites

Call: 092 233 5366Request a quote

LAND ING d.o.o. carries out engineering surveying on construction sites – from site preparation before construction, through setting-out and verticality checks, to as-built surveys. Our experienced team delivers the precision that is essential for the safety and quality of construction.

What is engineering surveying?

Engineering surveying covers all survey work related to the planning, construction and maintenance of structures. Its purpose is to transfer the design from paper to the ground precisely and to check the works at every stage – from the foundations to the roof.

Our construction-site services

  • Setting-out of buildings – transferring design axes and points to the ground
  • Setting-out of routes – roads, railways, gas, water and sewer lines
  • Construction control surveys – verticality, levelness and dimensions
  • As-built surveys – as-built documentation
  • Deformation and movement monitoring – of structures and ground
  • Volume surveys – excavations, embankments, stockpiles
  • Survey design – as part of the complete design documentation

Who we work for

  • construction companies and contractors
  • investors in residential and commercial projects
  • private clients building family houses
  • utility companies
  • state and local institutions
  • energy companies (wind farms, solar plants)

Our equipment

  • GNSS receivers
  • total stations with millimetre accuracy
  • 3D laser scanner for complex structures
  • drones for aerial photogrammetry
  • levels for height measurements
  • CAD software for processing and delivery

How we work together

  1. Project review – analysis of the survey requirements
  2. Quote – tailored to the project
  3. Setting-out – transferring the design to the ground
  4. Control surveys – throughout all stages of construction
  5. As-built survey – for handover
  6. Delivery – all documentation in CAD/PDF format
