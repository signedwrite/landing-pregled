Real estate brokerage

Real estate brokerage – buying and selling property

Call: 092 233 5366Request a quote

LAND ING d.o.o. offers professional brokerage for buying and selling property with an added advantage – as a surveying company we know the cadastral and legal status of every property in detail. A secure transaction, transparent documentation and quick resolution of all cadastral and land-register issues make us the right choice for your next purchase or sale.

Our advantage: surveying expertise in real estate

Unlike conventional agents, we also check the technical status of the property: consistency between the cadastre and land register, unregistered buildings, legalisation status, the history of subdivisions and possible boundary disputes. This saves you time and prevents unpleasant surprises after the purchase.

Services for sellers

  • market valuation of the property
  • check of the cadastral and legal status with proposals for alignment
  • professional photography and listing presentation
  • advertising on leading property portals
  • organising viewings and communication with buyers
  • negotiation and coordination with notaries

Services for buyers

  • property search based on your criteria
  • detailed check of the cadastre and land register before purchase
  • analysis of encumbrances, mortgages and liens
  • check of the building permit and legalisation status
  • price negotiation in your favour
  • assistance with contracts and payments

Selling with LAND ING

  1. Consultation and valuation – setting the optimal asking price
  2. Document check – cadastre, land register, permits
  3. Marketing – photos, description, listings
  4. Going to market – portals and our client base
  5. Viewings – with serious buyers
  6. Negotiation and contract – assistance up to signing at the notary
  7. Technical support after the sale – registration in the cadastre and land register

Types of property

  • family houses
  • flats
  • commercial premises
  • building land
  • agricultural land
  • holiday homes
  • industrial and logistics buildings

Commission and transparency

Our commission is clearly defined in the brokerage agreement, with no hidden costs. Clients receive a clear overview of all services before signing. Contact us for details – we prepare an individual offer for every case.

Why LAND ING d.o.o.?

  • experience with property in Slavonia and Dalmatia
  • fast and transparent communication
  • our own team for all surveying matters (legalisation, condominium division, subdivision)
  • professional handling of every transaction up to registration
