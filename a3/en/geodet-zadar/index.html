Frequently asked questions

How much does a survey report cost in Zadar?

Survey reports in Zadar start from 600 €. The final price depends on the type of report, the size of the parcel and the complexity of the job. Contact us for a free estimate.

How long does it take?

A standard survey report takes 14 to 30 days. Field work is done in 1–3 days; the rest is office processing and submission to the cadastre.

What do I need for condominium division in Zadar?

For condominium division you need the condominium division documentation, a co-ownership agreement and the occupancy permit of the building. Our team coordinates the whole process.

Do you resolve boundary issues in Zadar County?

Yes, boundary determination is one of our most requested services. We establish parcel boundaries by survey, prepare the report and carry out registration in the cadastre.
