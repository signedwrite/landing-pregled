LAND ING d.o.o. prepares survey reports for legalisation of family houses, farm and ancillary buildings, terraces, balconies and extensions. Our licensed surveying engineers prepare all the survey documentation required for the procedure under the Croatian Act on the Treatment of Illegally Constructed Buildings.

What is a survey report for legalisation?

The survey report for legalising an unpermitted building is a mandatory part of the documentation submitted with the application for a decision on the as-built state (legalisation decision). It proves the actual position, shape and size of the building on the cadastral parcel and is the basis for registering the building in the cadastre and land register after legalisation.

When do you need it?

  • to legalise a family house built without a building permit
  • to legalise ancillary buildings (garages, sheds, woodsheds)
  • to legalise extensions, additions and alterations
  • to legalise terraces, balconies and covered areas
  • to legalise farm buildings (stables, barns, greenhouses)
  • to legalise holiday homes and buildings on agricultural land

What does the report contain?

  • technical report on the measurements
  • survey plan of the actual situation
  • the building shown in the HTRS96/TM coordinate system
  • the building shown on the 2011 digital orthophoto (aerial image)

Procedure

  1. Consultation – review of the situation and documents
  2. Field survey – survey of the building and its position on the parcel
  3. Preparation of the report – in line with current regulations
  4. Submission to the cadastre – review and acceptance of the report
  5. Implementation in the cadastre – registration of the building after legalisation
  6. Implementation in the land register – registration of the building in the land register

Which documents are required?

  • cadastral parcel number and cadastral municipality
  • existing design documentation (if any)
  • photographs of the building (if required)
  • building and occupancy permits, if any

Cost

The price depends on the size and complexity of the building, the number of floors, the location and access to the parcel. Send us the parcel number, cadastral municipality and approximate dimensions of the building to office@landing.hr – we prepare a free quote the same day.
