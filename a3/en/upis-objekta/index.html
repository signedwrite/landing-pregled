LAND ING d.o.o. prepares survey reports for registering new and existing buildings in the real-estate cadastre and the land register. Our licensed engineers prepare the complete documentation – from the survey to registration – for family houses, residential buildings, farm buildings and ancillary structures.

What is a building registration survey report?

A survey report for recording a building is the document by which a building is entered in the real-estate cadastre and land register for the first time. It contains a precise survey of the actual position, shape and size of the building on the cadastral parcel and is a prerequisite for registering the building as legal real property.

When do you need to register a building?

  • to register a newly built house or building with an occupancy permit
  • after legalisation of an unpermitted building has been completed
  • to register an existing house that has never been recorded in the cadastre
  • when taking out a mortgage-backed loan
  • before selling or inheriting a house
  • to register ancillary buildings (garages, sheds, farm buildings)
  • to record an occupancy permit or legalisation decision in the title deed and cadastre

What does the report contain?

  • technical survey report
  • survey plan showing the building on the parcel
  • copy of the cadastral map showing the new situation
  • statement of building and parcel areas
  • coordinates of the building corners in the HTRS96/TM system
  • application for registration in the cadastre and land register

Registration procedure

  1. Consultation – review of documents (occupancy permit / legalisation decision)
  2. Field survey – survey of the building and its parcel
  3. Preparation of the report – in line with current regulations
  4. Submission to the cadastre – review and acceptance of the report
  5. Implementation in the cadastre – the building is entered in the cadastral records
  6. Implementation in the land register – the building is registered as part of the property

Documents you will need

  • cadastral parcel number and cadastral municipality
  • occupancy permit or legalisation decision (for legalised buildings)
  • building permit
  • architectural design documentation (if required)

Cost of building registration

The price depends on the size of the building, the number of buildings on the parcel, the location and access. For an exact quote, send us the parcel number, cadastral municipality and a short description of the building – we reply the same day.
