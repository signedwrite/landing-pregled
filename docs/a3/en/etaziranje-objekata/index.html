LAND ING d.o.o. prepares condominium division documentation (elaborat etažiranja) for residential and commercial buildings, family houses with several flats and mixed-use buildings. Our team of licensed surveying engineers and architects prepares all the documentation needed to register separate ownership (condominium ownership) in the land register.

What is condominium division?

Condominium division (etažiranje) is the procedure by which a building or part of a building is divided into independent units – flats, commercial premises, garages and other separate parts – that can be owned separately. The result is the registration of condominium ownership in the land register, giving each owner a separate title deed.

When do you need it?

  • when selling or buying a flat in a building that has not yet been divided
  • to register a flat or commercial unit in the land register
  • when taking out a mortgage on a flat
  • for inheritance and division of family houses with several units
  • for new developments – before selling flats to buyers
  • to regulate ownership in multi-unit residential buildings

What does the documentation contain?

  • technical description of the building and all separate units
  • floor plans of every storey with the separate units marked
  • calculation of the areas of all separate and common parts
  • calculation of the co-ownership shares for each unit
  • list of ancillary spaces (cellars, storage rooms, parking spaces) belonging to each unit
  • proposal for registration in the land register

Procedure

  1. Review of documents – building permit, occupancy permit and existing drawings
  2. Site visit – survey and check of the actual state of the building
  3. Drawings – floor plans of all storeys with the separate units precisely marked
  4. Areas and shares – calculated in line with the applicable methodology
  5. Notary – drafting of the co-ownership agreement
  6. Land register – final registration of condominium ownership

Which documents are required?

  • cadastral parcel number and cadastral municipality
  • building and occupancy permit (or legalisation decision)
  • existing architectural drawings of the building (if available)
  • agreement and consent of the co-owners

Cost

The price depends on the size of the building, the number of flats and separate units, the complexity of the floor plans and the availability of existing documentation.
