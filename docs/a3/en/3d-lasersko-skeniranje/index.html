3D laser scanning

3D laser scanning of buildings and terrain

Call: 092 233 5366Request a quote

LAND ING d.o.o. carries out 3D laser scanning of buildings, terrain, industrial plants and cultural monuments. Laser scanning produces a millimetre-accurate three-dimensional record of reality in the form of a point cloud, from which 3D models, sections, BIM models and other deliverables are produced for design, construction and conservation.

What is 3D laser scanning?

3D laser scanning (LiDAR) uses a laser beam to measure the distance from the scanner to every point in its surroundings. The result is a point cloud – hundreds of millions or billions of 3D coordinates describing the real space with millimetre accuracy. Architects, designers and contractors use the models derived from this data.

What we deliver

  • Point cloud – in standard formats (LAS, E57, RCP)
  • 3D model – processed and simplified model of the object
  • 2D drawings – floor plans, sections and elevations in CAD formats
  • Facade orthophotos – metrically accurate images of facades
  • Volume calculations – accurate to the cubic metre
  • 3D visualisations – for presentation and marketing

Typical applications

  • Existing buildings – for reconstruction or extension projects
  • Cultural and historic monuments – conservation documentation
  • Industrial plants – mapping of pipework and structures
  • Bridges and tunnels – engineering documentation and monitoring
  • Legal disputes – the precision required for expert witness reports
  • Forensics – recording incident scenes

Advantages of laser scanning

  • Speed – a complex building scanned in a few hours
  • Accuracy – ±2–5 mm at 25 metres
  • Non-contact – no risk of damaging sensitive objects
  • Completeness – everything is captured, nothing is missed
  • Documentation – a digital copy remains for future use
  • Fewer return visits – all information in a single scan

Scanning procedure

  1. Site inspection – analysis of the site, access and dimensions
  2. Scan planning – scanner positions and control points
  3. Field scanning – with the laser scanner
  4. Point cloud registration – joining individual scans into a whole
  5. Processing and modelling – producing the final deliverables
  6. Delivery – in the agreed formats

Cost

The price depends on the size and complexity of the object, the required deliverables and access to the site. Send us a description of the object and the purpose of the scan – we prepare an individual quote.
