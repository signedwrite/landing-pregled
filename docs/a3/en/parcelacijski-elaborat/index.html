What is a subdivision survey report?

A subdivision survey report (parcelacijski elaborat) is a survey document that changes the shape and size of cadastral parcels. It is most often prepared for:

  • splitting one parcel into several smaller ones
  • merging several parcels into one
  • changing the boundary between neighbouring parcels
  • forming a building plot for a building permit
  • implementing zoning and spatial plans

When do you need a subdivision survey?

You need a subdivision survey report in the following situations:

  • when selling part of your land (splitting a parcel)
  • when buying neighbouring land and merging it with yours
  • to form a building plot before building a house or other structure
  • to resolve a boundary between neighbours
  • for inheritance and division of family land
  • to implement a detailed zoning plan

How the survey is carried out

The process consists of the following steps:

  1. Consultation – discussion with the client and collection of basic documents
  2. Field survey – site visit and identification of parcel boundaries
  3. Preparation of the report – in line with the Ordinance on survey reports
  4. Submission to the cadastre – to the competent regional cadastral office
  5. Implementation in the cadastre – updating of the cadastral records
  6. Implementation in the land register – transfer of the data to the land register (if required)

Which documents are required?

The following are usually required:

  • cadastral parcel number and cadastral municipality
  • a final location/building conditions decision (when forming a building plot)
  • consent of co-owners (for co-owned parcels)

Cost of a subdivision survey

The price depends on several factors: the size and number of parcels, the number of boundary points, the location, existing boundary markers in the field and the complexity of the cadastral procedure.
