Frequently asked questions

How much does a survey report cost in Osijek?

Survey reports in Osijek start from from 600 €. The final price depends on the type of report, the size of the parcel and the complexity of the job. Contact us for a free estimate.

How long does it take?

A standard survey report takes 7 to 14 working days. Field work is done in 1–3 days; the rest is office processing and submission to the cadastre.

What do I need for condominium division in Osijek?

For condominium division you need the condominium division documentation, a co-ownership agreement and the occupancy permit of the building. Our team coordinates the whole process.

What is condominium division and when do I need it?

Condominium division establishes separate ownership of individual parts of a building (flat, commercial unit, garage). You need it when buying a flat, taking out a loan or registering ownership of a flat.
