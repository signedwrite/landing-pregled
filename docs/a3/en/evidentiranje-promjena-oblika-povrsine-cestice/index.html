LAND ING d.o.o. prepares survey reports for recording changes in the shape or area of cadastral parcels. Such a report is needed when the data in the cadastre do not match the actual situation on the ground – that is, when the real shape and area of a parcel must be aligned with the real-estate cadastre.

What is this survey report?

It is the document by which changes in the actual shape and area of a cadastral parcel are entered in the cadastre and land register. It is prepared in line with the applicable Ordinance and serves as the basis for updating the cadastral records.

When do you need it?

  • when the parcel area measured on site differs from the cadastre
  • when the shape of a parcel has changed (e.g. due to road construction or river regulation)
  • to align cadastral data with reality before a sale
  • to correct errors from old surveys
  • to settle relations with neighbours
  • to register the new position of boundary points

What does the report contain?

  • technical report describing the survey procedure
  • survey plan of the actual situation
  • old and new parcel boundaries
  • statement of old and new areas
  • coordinates of all boundary points in the HTRS96/TM system
  • application form for registering the changes in the cadastre

Procedure

  1. Consultation – analysis of cadastral data and discussion with the client
  2. Field survey – survey of actual boundaries and boundary points
  3. Comparison with the cadastre – analysis of discrepancies
  4. Preparation of the report – in line with the Ordinance on survey reports
  5. Submission to the cadastre – review and acceptance
  6. Implementation – update of the cadastral records and transfer to the land register

Required documents

  • cadastral parcel number and cadastral municipality

Cost

The price depends on the size of the parcel, the number of boundary points, the location and access. For an exact quote, send us the parcel number and cadastral municipality – we reply the same day.
