LAND ING d.o.o. prepares topographic surveys as the base map for designing family houses, residential buildings, farm buildings and infrastructure. A topographic survey shows the actual state of the site precisely – terrain, existing buildings, utilities, parcel boundaries and spot heights – and is a mandatory basis for any design.

What is a topographic survey?

A topographic survey (geodetska situacija) is a graphic representation of the surveyed area at a given scale (usually 1:200, 1:500 or 1:1000) showing all relevant buildings, infrastructure, vegetation and topography. It is produced digitally (DWG, DXF) and handed over to architects and engineers as the basis for design.

When do you need it?

  • to design a family house or building
  • as a basis for a location permit
  • to design the reconstruction or extension of an existing building
  • to design roads, car parks and infrastructure
  • to design gardens, pools and landscaping
  • for utility connection conditions

What does it contain?

  • boundaries of cadastral parcels and neighbouring buildings
  • existing buildings on the parcel (buildings, outbuildings, fences)
  • utilities (water, sewerage, electricity, gas)
  • roads, accesses and footpaths
  • spot heights and contour lines
  • vegetation – trees, hedges, green areas
  • coordinates in the HTRS96/TM system

Procedure

  1. Consultation – discussion with the client and designer, defining the survey scope
  2. Field survey – GNSS and total station, drone if required
  3. Data processing – in a CAD environment (AutoCAD, Civil 3D)
  4. Drafting – graphic representation at the required scale
  5. Delivery – in digital (DWG/DXF/PDF) and printed form

Cost

The price depends on the size of the area, the density of features, the scale and the designer's requirements. For an exact quote, send us the parcel number, cadastral municipality and purpose of the survey (e.g. house design, reconstruction, utility connections).
